How a build actually runs

From diagnostic call to cohort report

  1. Diagnostic call. A 45-minute conversation on what the workforce needs to do differently, and what's stopping them now.
  2. Material review. For programmes over 20 seats, we review real workflows, tickets or case material so examples aren't generic.
  3. Curriculum build. Modules, assessment questions and facilitation guides drafted and reviewed with the client's subject owner.
  4. Pilot cohort. A first run, often smaller, with a debrief before the calendar opens to the rest of the group.
  5. Full delivery. Scheduled cohorts through to completion, in person or live online.
  6. Cohort report. Assessment results, completion rates and facilitator notes sent back to the sponsoring team.
