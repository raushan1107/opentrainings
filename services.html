How we work

Trainers, labs and vouchers, run as one process

Most engagements use several of these in sequence: a diagnostic call, a trainer match, a delivery run with its lab environment, and vouchers issued at the end. A few clients only need one piece. Both are normal.

How engagements are structured

Three ways to bring us in

No packaged tiers with one plan marked as the popular choice. The right model depends on group size and whether this repeats annually.

How a build actually runs

From diagnostic call to cohort report

  1. Diagnostic call. A 45-minute conversation on what the workforce needs to do differently, and what's stopping them now.
  2. Trainer match. A facilitator with real field experience in the subject is matched to the brief, and a lab environment or vouchers are lined up if the track needs one.
  3. Curriculum build. Modules, assessment questions and facilitation guides drafted and reviewed with the client's subject owner.
  4. Pilot cohort. A first run, often smaller, with a debrief before the calendar opens to the rest of the group.
  5. Full delivery. Scheduled cohorts through to completion, in person or live online.
  6. Cohort report. Assessment results, completion rates and facilitator notes sent back to the sponsoring team.
