Training Infra

The room, the travel, the rest — arranged end to end

Classroom or venue, travel and stay, food, and workspace, arranged around your dates and headcount. Bring your own trainer, take one of ours, or send someone else entirely — the logistics work the same way.

What's arranged

Everything except the content

Plenty of teams already have a trainer, a curriculum or a certification partner lined up, and just need the logistics around it handled: a room, a way to get there, somewhere to eat, somewhere to sit and work. That's a booking on its own, no training programme required.

Ask about arranging your dates
  • Seating and classroom layout for the confirmed headcount
  • Projector, screen, whiteboards and Wi-Fi checked before day one
  • Travel and stay coordinated for a trainer or attendees flying in
  • Meals and refreshment logistics for single or multi-day sessions
  • Workspace set up for a trainer or staff embedded on-site longer term
  • A sign-in desk for compliance and attendance-sensitive cohorts

It's not just for our own trainers

Your trainer, an examiner, an auditor — same arrangement either way

This isn't tied to booking one of our programmes. If someone needs to be on-site to run or observe a session, whether that's your own facilitator, a certification body's examiner, or an internal auditor, we'll arrange the room, the travel and the rest around their visit the same way.

Three ways to arrange it

Client site, our hub, or an external venue

Most in-house programmes use one of these three. All three work whether the trainer is ours or yours.
